Owner (S354, 2026-07-10) wants reviewers' institutions added to their CRM
contacts to stop the recurring `reviewer_contact_affiliation_mismatch` System
Alert (Admin → System Alerts). Alert is generated by
`lib/services/alert-reviewer-affiliation-mismatch.js` (fired from
`lib/services/reviewer-acceptance-drain.js` on accept) — it deliberately does
NOT auto-write; free-text affiliations have variants/acronyms/AKAs unsafe to
auto-resolve. Compares the reviewer's reported affiliation
(`wmkf_revieweraffiliation` on the accepted suggestion, or `contactEdits.affiliation`)
to the linked contact's institution (`_parentcustomerid_value` account link, or
`adx_organizationname`).

**Read-only probe run 2026-07-10** (`scripts/probe-reviewer-affiliation-account-match.js`,
reads `system_alerts` PG + Dataverse `accounts` + accepted `wmkf_appreviewersuggestions`;
no writes). Findings:
- Live backlog is **8** active/acknowledged alerts (NOT hundreds); 21 accepted
  reviewers total have a reported affiliation. ALL 8 are the "contact has NO
  institution" case — zero are real conflicts (different institution).
- Only **~30%** (6/21; 3/8 backlog) exact-match exactly one Account → the only
  safe auto-link subset. **~62%** match NO account at all (institution isn't in
  the CRM under a matching name — e.g. "Washington University School of Medicine"
  when the account is "Washington University in St. Louis"; Oxford, Mass Chan
  absent). 120 of 4629 accounts are duplicate normalized names. Affiliation data
  is noisy ("testing", "Dr.", full postal strings).

**Root cause = messy account data, not matching.** Missing + duplicated Account
targets, so no matching cleverness fixes it.

**Design options discussed (all PARKED, nothing built):**
1. Auto-link only the exact-single-match subset (~30%). Low yield.
2. Auto-write reported affiliation to free-text `adx_organizationname` on accept
   (fill-only) — safe, 100% coverage, silences alert, but NOT the structured
   Company (`parentcustomerid`) lookup staff normally see.
3. Suppress the pure-absence alert; keep alerting only on true conflicts.
4. **Institution typeahead backed by an AKA dictionary** (owner's preferred
   direction). Pieces already in repo: `OpenAlexService.searchInstitutions()`
   (openalex-service.js:479) → OpenAlex institutions carry `display_name` +
   `display_name_alternatives` (aliases) + `display_name_acronyms` + `ror` +
   country = a ready canonical+AKA dictionary. Cache/refresh precedent =
   `pages/api/cron/refresh-irs-bmf.js` (external dataset → PG staging table →
   atomic swap, used for name matching).

**Key catch:** a typeahead resolves to a *canonical institution identity* (ROR),
NOT to a CRM Account. Setting `parentcustomerid` still needs an Account, and 62%
don't exist → choose free-text/ROR identity write vs auto-create Accounts from
ROR (safer than free-text since ROR-keyed dedup, but a governance decision to
mint hundreds of accounts). Cache-vs-Dataverse note: typeahead→OpenAlex never
touches Dataverse (no cache needed for latency); typeahead→your-Accounts is
where a cached local copy avoids per-keystroke Dataverse slowness.

**Owner insight (S354) that settles the architecture — TWO NAMESPACES:**
OpenAlex/ROR hold *research-affiliation* names ("University of California, Los
Angeles", "UCLA"); CRM Accounts hold *legal/payee* names ("The Regents of the
University of California") because that's who contracts/honoraria pay. OpenAlex
will NEVER carry the legal payee form, so it can't be the resolution TARGET — the
typeahead must always resolve against Dataverse Accounts (owner's original
"cache because Dataverse is slow" instinct = cache a local copy of the Accounts).
OpenAlex's real role shrinks to an ALIAS FEEDER: attach research-name variants +
a stable key to each Account. So the concrete cleanup deliverable (for Connor +
Sarah) is: **enrich each Account with a ROR id and/or EIN + known aliases**, so
research-name input ("UCLA") bridges to the legal Account. Raw material already
in-repo for both namespaces: OpenAlex/ROR = research aliases/acronyms; IRS BMF
(cached by `pages/api/cron/refresh-irs-bmf.js`, table `irs_exempt_orgs`) = LEGAL
nonprofit names + EINs (same namespace as Accounts; natural key for the missing
62%). Crosswalk = reviewer research name → OpenAlex/ROR aliases → ROR/EIN key →
legal Account. This is why account cleanup is the unlock and why it's reusable.

**Owner decision (S354):** take this to **Connor + Sarah** — they acknowledge the
Dynamics DB is a mess and account cleanup is worth doing anyway. Sequencing:
canonicalize/dedupe accounts (ROR spine) FIRST, then reviewer→Account linking is
near-trivial and REUSABLE (same canonicalization helps reviewer COI institution
match, `save-candidates` account lookup, intake institution match). Do NOT build
the typeahead/cache before the account cleanup — it paints over messy targets.
Justified as shared institution-resolution infrastructure, not an 8-alert fix.
