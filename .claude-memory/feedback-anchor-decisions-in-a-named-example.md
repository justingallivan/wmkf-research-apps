## Recall Rule
Read before asking the owner to choose between rules, thresholds or evidence levels (AskUserQuestion or chat).

Do: say who the rule applies to with a named real example from the work at hand (for example "Yifan Cheng on request 1003526"), what the program director clicks or types under each option, and only then the recommendation.
Do not: open with the mechanism ("the stored email equals the applicant-listed email by construction") or with labels coined during the session.

**Why:** owner, 2026-11-20, rejecting an abstract evidence-link question about applicant-suggested reviewers: "I don't understand. Who are we talking about here?" The same question, restated around one reviewer and the three things a director would do ("one click", "checkbox", "paste a link"), got a decision right away, and the follow-up "explain the practical differences" asked for the same thing.

**How to apply:** for each option, describe the observable experience first and the internals second, briefly. Related: [[feedback-no-item-codes-in-owner-facing-text]], [[feedback-user-facing-error-copy-voice]].
