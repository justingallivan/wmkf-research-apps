Executive Summary, Methodology, Personnel, Rationale, Flags, Keywords) or its
output limits.

Do: start from the workbook requirements extracted in

experimental limits.

flow is in `docs/plans/PA_PROMPT_DELIVERY_HANDOFF_2026-11-16.md`.

## Context

The J27 Review Workbook mockup (`J27_Review_Workbook_Mockup_v10.1 (edited by SH, JG, CN, DS 11.10.26).xlsx`, sheet `1 Review (staff master)`; requirements extracted in `docs/plans/PREFLIGHT_PROMPTS_HANDOFF_2026-11-10.md`, copied to main 2026-11-19) represents what Beth, the CSO, has in mind for the pre-flight/intake prompt outputs (Short Title, Executive Summary, Methodology, Personnel, Rationale, Flags, Keywords, Open questions, Staff notes).

**Why:** owner said: "The workbook is what Beth, the CSO has in mind. We should keep that in mind. Her word is not canon but we have to take her thought into account."

**How to apply:** when drafting or revising intake prompts, start from the workbook's requirements (lengths, outputs, flags, audiences) and call out every place a draft departs from it, so the owner can decide knowingly. Do not treat the workbook as final: the owner decides, and some items (Chairs audiences, keyword lists, flag rubric) are explicitly provisional. Never silently override it with earlier experimental limits either. Related: [[feedback-keep-the-owners-named-input-source]].
