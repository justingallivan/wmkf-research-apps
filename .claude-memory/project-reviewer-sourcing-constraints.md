## Recall Rule

Read this before any reviewer-finder design that ranks candidate SOURCES,
proposes pool/roster reuse, elevates applicant-suggested reviewers, or plans
"reviewer network" features.

## The facts (owner-stated, Justin, 2026-07-08)

1. **Applicant-suggested reviewers are capped at ~1 per panel** in practice, to
   avoid undue influence by potential friends-of-applicant. High quality (80%
   blind PD pick-rate in the S246 origination experiment) but policy-limited —
   never a primary slate channel. The friends-of-PI *bias* was already recorded
   (`docs/REVIEWER_FINDER_RETRIEVAL_REDESIGN_PLAN.md` Part C §8); the operational
   cap was not, until now.
2. **No reviewer has been re-used in ~4 years.** Reasons: (a) sparse data —
   improving with the current legibility work; (b) staff avoid wearing out past
   reviewers' welcome; (c) field breadth is so wide there is rarely a known
   expert group on tap. This CONTRADICTS `docs/STRATEGY.md:81` ("good reviewers
   get called upon repeatedly") — strategy-doc fix pending.
3. Consequence: **every proposal is a cold start by portfolio nature.** The
   Claude-assisted origination engine is the product (fills nearly every panel
   seat), referral capture is the only human channel compatible with these
   constraints, and applicant recommendations are worth more as community
   *search seeds* than as candidates.

**Why:** the 2026-07-08 holistic review
(`docs/audits/reviewer-holistic-review-fable-2026-07-08.md`) initially
recommended a roster flywheel and applicant-recs-first slate from the repo
record alone; the owner corrected it same-day. These practice facts are not
derivable from code or docs — do not re-derive designs that assume reuse.

**How to apply:** score finding investments by (a) per-proposal engine quality,
(b) referral capture, (c) applicant-recs-as-seeds — never by pool reuse or
per-person reviewer history. Related:
[[project-reviewer-recall-over-precision]],
