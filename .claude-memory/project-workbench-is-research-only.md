## Recall Rule
Read before adding a J27 program gate, widening a Research-only Workbench feature to Southern California, or planning anything "cross-program" in the Workbench.

**Decision (Justin, 2026-11-09):** the J27 read model, its stage strip and its gates apply to the Research grant program only (`deriveJ27ForRow` requires the readiness-only scope resolved by catalog name). Justin: "The more we do on the app for the research program, the harder it will be to make it directly compatible with SoCal. I'm actually fine with that as their business practices differ." If a SoCal suite is wanted later it is built as its own app, reusing code and lessons.

**Why:** the Research workflow (First Cut, Semi-finalist, Chairs, reviewer invitations, presentations) has no SoCal counterpart; a cycle-year-only guard would have shown Research tasks on the 87 production J27 SoCal requests.

**How to apply:** keep the program gate; do not add SoCal branches to Research surfaces; log any SoCal need as a separate app idea. Recorded as D8 in the read-model plan (`J27_READ_MODEL_AND_DISCOVERY_PLAN_2026-11-08`, a plans document on branch `claude/j27-read-model`, not yet on main).
