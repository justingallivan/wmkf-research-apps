## Direction retained

Use full narrative and bibliography to identify a small set of consequential scientific judgments, then search for supported complementary contributions. Critical does not mean low risk or already demonstrated. Preserve identity/conflict/contact checks and human selection. Secure critical coverage, avoid concentrating all critical judgments in one person, and add purposeful independent overlap and supporting breadth. Judge improvement by a concrete panel contribution, not a larger candidate list or aggregate percentage alone.

Full paper text is best effort; abstracts may support fit when sufficient. Missing full text is uncertainty, not automatic exclusion. Paper authorship and identity verification do not by themselves prove a person's contribution to a judgment.

## Resume boundary

First consider one staff-assisted practice report and measure correction/review effort. No application has been selected, no pilot has run, and no implementation or metered run is authorized. Only after that practice case should the proposed six-case comparison or a small UI change be considered. Exact input availability, field mappings, resource budgets and operational arrangements remain unverified/unsettled.

Potential first product slice, if later justified and authorized: editable judgments; candidate contribution with evidence and uncertainty; staff-triggered search for one gap. Defer automatic panel selection, exhaustive citation expansion, global expertise profiles and automatic follow-up searches.
