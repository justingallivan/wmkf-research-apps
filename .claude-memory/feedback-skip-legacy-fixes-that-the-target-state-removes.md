## Recall Rule

Read this when: you notice a legacy label ("Pre-Site Visit"), a redundant checkbox, a
copy leak, or an interim affordance in a surface whose target design is already decided
and close to shipping (Staff Deliberations tab, briefing page, Share composer).

**Owner (2026-09-10):** "Let's just move through the work and not correct legacy issues
that will go away when we deliver something close to the final product."

Do:
- Name the legacy issue in one line if it matters for understanding, then keep building
  the target shape (e.g. materials on the briefing page instead of trimming the
  material-link checkboxes one at a time).
- Queue only work that survives into the target product.

Do not:
- Offer or build copy relabels, interim trims, or partial cleanups of surfaces that the
  decided design replaces.
- Ask the owner to choose between "fix the symptom" and "build the target" — build the
  target unless it is blocked.

**Why:** the owner's time is the constraint; interim fixes cost review cycles and vanish.
