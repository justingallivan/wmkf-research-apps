- **Templates already aligned:** the deterministic proposal-discussion-brief template has `Issues to be Addressed at the Research Presentation:`. Meeting-summary section headings (`What was presented`, `Main points raised`, etc.) are neutral. No fixed-heading rename was identified there.
- **Stable integration language:** routes, setting/preference keys, prompt names, numeric artifact types, `Artifacts/Pre-Site Visit`, `Site Visit - Slides`, snapshot schema fields, `[[STAFF:ScientificPresentation]]`, and `Stopped by the Cycle Dossier operator.` remain contracts/history.
