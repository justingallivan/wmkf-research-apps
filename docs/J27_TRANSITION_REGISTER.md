# J27 Transition Register

**What this is.** The single list of sites that change meaning, retire, or must be built when the foundation moves from the last dual-phase cycle (D26, December 2026 board) to the first single-submission cycle (J27, June 2027 board). Method, schema, and categories are defined in `docs/J27_SINGLE_PHASE_TRANSITION_INVENTORY_PLAN.md`. This register is the sweep's deliverable; the build plan is a later, separate document.

**How it was built.** Six read-only agents swept disjoint slices of the repo on 2026-09-06 (lib · pages and shared components · config, scripts, tests, migrations · top-level docs · Atlas, wiki, plans, audits · memory and history). The session merged their reports, deduplicated sites that appeared in more than one slice, and downgraded labels the excerpt did not support. Slice G (Dataverse-resident prompts, email templates, Power Automate flows) is owner-run and appears as a checklist in §8. Line numbers are as of commit `d05af5e8` and will drift; the excerpt is the durable locator.

**Rules.**
- Nothing here is scheduled. Every row is `open`, `rejected (…)`, or `done (…)`. A row moves to `scheduled` only by a work-queue entry, and only if its label is `[OWNER-CONFIRMED]` or `[SOURCE-VERIFIED]`.
- **Retire rows are candidates, not authorizations.** Each gets a live-caller grep and a read of the likely load-bearing paths at execution time, per the destructive-carryover rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
- `[ASSUMED]` rows record speculation so it is visible, not so it is acted on.
- Calendar: J27 proposals arrive in early December 2026, exact date TBD (owner, 2026-09-06). The 2026-08-18 date that older text carries was the D26 Phase II proposal due date. **D26 is in its reviewer season now** (invitations and acceptances done, reviews arriving; 22 D26 reviews backfilled 2026-09-04); the D26 board meets December 2026. J27 reviewer invitations follow in 2027. Anything phrased "before D26 invitations" is a dating error.

Legend: **Dep** = cycle dependency (`D26` = D26-only, `dual` = dual-phase-only, `both` = both cycles, `J27` = J27-only, `?` = unknown). **Ev** = evidence label (`OC` = [OWNER-CONFIRMED], `SV` = [SOURCE-VERIFIED], `AS` = [ASSUMED]). **Q** = owner question in §7, or `–`.
