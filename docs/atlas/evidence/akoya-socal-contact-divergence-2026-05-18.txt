REDACTED AGGREGATE EVIDENCE
Probe date: 2026-05-18 (read-only production verification)
Subject: request-primary-contact versus organization-primary-contact divergence

Population and result
---------------------
- 2025-cycle requests in the scoped grant program: 268.
- Rows with both contact lookups populated: 228.
- Rows where request and organization primary contacts differed: 71.
- Divergence rate among rows with both contacts: approximately 31%.

A 15-row sample was inspected to confirm that the difference was substantive,
not a formatting or lookup-annotation artifact. The sample also showed that
organization leader/CEO, organization primary contact, and request primary
contact are separate roles and may coincide or diverge independently.

Conclusion
----------
The request-level primary contact cannot be substituted with the applicant
organization's primary contact. Consumers must select the role that matches
their workflow rather than treating either lookup as a universal person.

Redaction boundary
------------------
The original output contained the grant-program GUID and row-level request
numbers, meeting dates, organizations, contact/leader names, and contact
GUIDs. Those production-derived values are intentionally excluded. Re-run the
read-only probe for any authorized row-level investigation.
