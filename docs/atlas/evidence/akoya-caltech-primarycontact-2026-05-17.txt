REDACTED AGGREGATE EVIDENCE
Probe date: 2026-05-17 (read-only production verification)
Subject: primary-contact coverage for one research-institution cohort

Discovery
---------
The organization-name/alias search returned two applicant accounts. Account
GUIDs and exact organization names are intentionally omitted.

Aggregate observations
----------------------
- Requests in the cohort: 265.
- Requests with `akoya_primarycontactid`: 122/265 (46%).
- Migrated requests with a primary contact: 96/234.
- Native requests with a primary contact: 26/31.

A newest-by-decision-date spot-check showed that many native requests shared
the same recurring institutional primary contact, including requests whose
titles represented distinct research projects. Some requests had no primary
contact; a small historical migrated sample used a different institutional
contact.

Conclusion
----------
`akoya_primarycontactid` is useful for institutional correspondence but is not
a reliable principal-investigator field. Native coverage was much higher than
migrated coverage in this cohort, reinforcing the era-specific semantics.

Redaction boundary
------------------
The original probe output included account GUIDs, request numbers, dates,
statuses, proposal-title fragments, and contact names. They are not required
to preserve the field-semantics conclusion and are intentionally excluded.
Re-run the read-only production probe when row-level evidence is needed.
