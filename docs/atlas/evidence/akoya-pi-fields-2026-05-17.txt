The 384 populated `wmkf_researchleader` rows were confined to three program

Institutional spot-check
------------------------
Two native-request institutional cohorts were inspected. In both, the request
primary contact frequently remained the same recurring institutional liaison
while project/research leaders varied with the proposed work. That separation
supports modeling the principal investigator independently from
`akoya_primarycontactid`.

Conclusion
----------
`wmkf_researchleader` and `wmkf_projectleader` are candidate PI fields;
`akoya_primarycontactid` is not a safe default PI field because it commonly
represents an institutional liaison.

------------------

names, and people. Those row-level production values are intentionally
excluded. Re-run the dated read-only probe for row-level substantiation.
