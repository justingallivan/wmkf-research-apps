# Akoya reviewer linkage probe — redacted evidence summary

Original probe time: 2026-05-17T02:26:55.461Z
Probe mode: read-only
Original evidence commit: 09e81fdb (2026-05-16)
Current-tree redaction: 2026-07-27

## Scope and aggregate result

- Sampled honorarium/reviewer requests: 5 of an 87-request cohort.
- Distinct Dataverse contacts resolved from the 5 requests: 5.
- Contact state: 3 active and 2 inactive.
- Parent-account linkage: 0 of 5 contacts had a parent account.
- All 5 requests had both a GoApply-submitter relationship and a
  primary-contact relationship.
- One sampled request also had populated Bill.com address, organization, and
  payment-network fields; it was the staff/test-like row in the sample.

## Useful linkage fields retained

The probe established that the reviewer person can be followed through these
request relationships:

- `_akoya_goapplyapplication_value`
- `_akoya_goapplyphase_value`
- `_akoya_goapplysettings_value`
- `_akoya_goapplysubmitter_value`
- `_akoya_primarycontactid_value`
- program and owner lookups

The original request dump also included request-state, honorarium, payee, and
Bill.com field groups. Those values are not needed to preserve the linkage
conclusion.

## Conclusion

The reviewer person already resolves to a Dataverse `contact`. Review-manager
to Dataverse integration should therefore key person linkage on the existing
contact rather than introduce a second person entity. That contact identity
still needs reconciliation with the reviewer pool, Postgres researcher
records, and the program-director resolver.

## Redaction and reproducibility boundary

The original sample contained person names, email addresses, request/contact
identifiers, mailing addresses, and payment-network identifiers. Those values
have been removed from the current tree. No hashes or stable aliases derived
from them are retained.

Exact row-level reproduction requires rerunning the controlled read-only
Dataverse probe under an authorized data-access context. Future tracked
evidence should record aggregate linkage states and field names, not raw
person-level values.
