payment-control fields, while Bill.com account details are sparse: 8/87 rows
overall and 7/85 external-reviewer rows for most Bill.com fields. Population of
a control/default field is not evidence that a payment was issued or that its
value is operationally meaningful.

## Redaction and reproducibility boundary

The original evidence included row-level examples containing request
identifiers, email addresses, mailing addresses, organization names, and
payment-network identifiers. Those examples have been removed from the current
tree. The aggregate counts and field names above are retained; no hashes or
opaque derivatives of the removed values remain.

Exact row-level verification requires rerunning the controlled read-only
Dataverse probe under an authorized data-access context. Future tracked
evidence should retain this aggregate form.
