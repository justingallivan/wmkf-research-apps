## Scope

- Honorarium/reviewer cohort: 87 requests.
- Requests with at least one Bill.com-related field populated: 8.
- Of those 8, 1 was classified as staff/test by the original probe and 7 were
  external-reviewer rows.

## Aggregate result

- All 8 populated rows had `akoya_requeststatus = Pending`.
- All 8 populated rows had `akoya_paid = 0`.
- The populated rows clustered near the earliest creation dates in the cohort.
  This supports the original interpretation that the Bill.com-populated rows
  were setup/test-era records, but the date clustering alone does not prove
  that every row was synthetic.

The probe inspected these useful field groups:

- Request state: `akoya_requestnum`, `createdon`,
  `akoya_requeststatus`, `akoya_paid`.
- Reviewer linkage: primary-contact and GoApply-submitter relationships.
- Bill.com/payee data:
  `wmkf_organizationnameonbillcomaccount`,
  `wmkf_paymentnetworkidpni`, `wmkf_usingpayee`, and the Bill.com
  email/address fields.

## Redaction and reproducibility boundary

The original row-level dump contained request identifiers, person names,
email addresses, mailing addresses, and payment-network identifiers. Those
values have been removed from the current tree. No hashes or opaque
derivatives of those values are retained here.

The aggregate counts, field names, probe timestamp, and original interpretation
remain reproducible. Exact row-level verification requires rerunning the
controlled read-only Dataverse probe under an authorized data-access context;
the raw values should not be copied into tracked documentation.
